About the game

The sky broke. What is left is a scatter of voxel islands drifting over open air, each one a different biome, each one with something worth taking.

Sky Forge is a survival adventure with the blocky look and hands-on building of Minecraft, and the long progression of Ark. You start with your bare hands and a patch of grass. You finish with a fortified base, forged gear and the strength to clear the dungeons buried inside the islands.

Key features

Floating islands

Hop between shattered islands, each with its own resources, hazards and secrets.

Ark-style progression

Level up, unlock new recipes and work your way from wooden tools to powerful late-game gear.

Dungeons

Descend into dungeons for tougher fights and better loot than the surface can offer.

Mine and build

Break, place and craft in a fully voxel world. Shape your base any way you like.

Survive

Manage hunger, danger and the long drop below. One bad step and you fall.
